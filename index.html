How to play

Find the four hidden words. The top and bottom words read left to right, the left and right words read top to bottom. The corner letters are given.

  • Drag a letter onto a square, or tap a letter and then a square.
  • Drag a placed letter to another square to swap, or off the board to remove it.
  • On a keyboard: type letters to fill the highlighted square, Backspace to remove, arrows to move, Enter to submit.
  • Stuck? A hint reveals one letter. Try to solve it with as few hints as possible.

A new daily puzzle appears every day at midnight. Share your result and challenge your friends!
